## Maplewood Asset I Consider Highest Risk

The most important asset is the Electronic Health Record, EHR, because it contains highly sensitive, personalized identifiable information, PII, 
that cannot simply be replaced, creating significant confidentiality and privacy risks if exposed. 
Additionally, maintaining the integrity of the EHR is critical because changes to medical information, such as allergies or medical history, 
could lead to incorrect treatment and potentially life threatening consequences

informed decisions.
